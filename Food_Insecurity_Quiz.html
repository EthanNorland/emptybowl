Study Reference

Key definitions from the USDA HFSSM and Gretchen Swanson Center for Nutrition.

Food Insecurity: Limited or uncertain access to enough food for an active, healthy life due to limited money or resources.
Marginal Food Security: 1–2 affirmative items (food anxiety); lumped with "food secure" in standard U.S. counts despite intermediate child and maternal health risks.
Nutrition Security: Nutritional adequacy of accessible food plus ability to meet cultural, religious, allergy, and taste needs.
L.A. County Sample (Dec 2022): Understanding America Study, N = 1,071 adults; 23.5% food insecure, 24.5% nutrition insecure, 13.5% both.
