Systemic Framework

The Hidden Arithmetic of Hunger

Food insecurity is rarely a total absence of food; it is the constant, grinding uncertainty of where the next meal will come from, forced trade-offs between groceries and shelter, and the chronic depletion of nutritional health.

Common Misconception vs. Reality

Over 70% of households visiting community food pantries include at least one working adult. Hunger is not a personal failure or confined to unhoused populations—it is the direct outcome of wages failing to meet escalating living costs.

Official Definition: "A lack of consistent access to enough food for every person in a household to live an active, healthy life." Source: United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Economic Research Service
